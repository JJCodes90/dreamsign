# Research behind the design

Short notes on which findings shaped which decisions. The sources are also listed in the app under **Settings › The science**.

| Finding | Source | What Dreamsign does |
|---|---|---|
| Keeping a dream logbook is associated with higher dream recall than retrospective estimates. | Aspy, Delfabbro & Proeve (2015), *Consciousness and Cognition* 33, 364–374 | The journal is a dismissal challenge, so writing happens every morning. Entries save automatically, and drafts survive the app being killed. |
| Recall fades fast after waking, and distraction erodes it. | Common to the dream-recall literature; see the review above | The default order is Shake → Journal. The editor shows a tip when Math comes before Journal. |
| Sleep inertia: performance is impaired for roughly the first 15–30 min after waking. Speed suffers at least as much as accuracy. | Hilditch & McHill (2019), *Nature and Science of Sleep* 11, 155–165 | No time limits on challenges. 64–72 pt targets, a big custom keypad, auto-submit, and no tiny controls. Shake (physical activation) goes first. |
| MILD works about as well as SSILD. Better general dream recall, and falling asleep soon after doing the techniques (within ~10 min), predicted success. | Aspy (2020), *Frontiers in Psychology* 11, 1746 (ILDIS) | The WBTB flow is deliberately short: a 10-word journal, a 1–5 min MILD rehearsal, then a dim back-to-sleep screen that lets the phone lock. |
| Reality testing alone did less than MILD. | Aspy, Delfabbro, Proeve & Mohr (2017), *Dreaming* 27(3), 206–231 | No daytime reality-check nagging. MILD is the core technique. |
| REM periods lengthen in the second half of the night, and wake-back-to-bed protocols typically wake people 4½–6 h after lights-out. | Stumbrys et al. (2012), *Consciousness and Cognition* 21(3), 1456–1475 (review); standard sleep architecture | The WBTB offset is 4½–6 h in 15-minute steps, defaulting to 5 h. |
| Dream signs: recurring, odd elements of your dreams are the best cues for becoming lucid. MILD visualizes spotting one. | LaBerge & Rheingold (1990), *Exploring the World of Lucid Dreaming* | Manual tagging of people, places and themes, the Top dream signs view, and the MILD "Recall" phase quotes your own entry. |

## MILD script used in the app

Phases rotate every 20 s for the chosen duration (default 3 min):

1. **Recall**: replay the dream you just wrote down. Your entry is shown.
2. **Intend**: *"Next time I'm dreaming, I'll recognize I'm dreaming."* Said slowly to yourself.
3. **Visualize**: see yourself back in that dream, notice something odd, and realize it's a dream.

The breathing orb paces about 5.5 s in and 5.5 s out, a slow, calming rhythm.

## Not claimed

The app makes no promises about lucid-dream rates. Published success rates vary widely between studies and people. The design follows the direction of the evidence rather than any single number.
